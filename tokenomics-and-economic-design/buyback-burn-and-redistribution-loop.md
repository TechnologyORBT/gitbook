# Buyback, Burn & Redistribution Loop

Over time, **governance may direct protocol surplus** (from fees, yields, facilitator profits, etc.) toward **buyback and burn mechanisms**.\
These funds repurchase ORBT from the market and either **burn** them or **redistribute** them to **stakers via USM**.

This loop supports:

* **Token scarcity and deflationary pressure.**
* **Value alignment** between holders, facilitators, and governance.
* **Sustainable yield** for active stakers.
* **Continuous treasury recycling** into protocol health.

The buyback and burn cycle forms the **core of ORBT’s long-term value flywheel**, ensuring that as protocol usage grows, the token’s scarcity and governance influence both strengthen.

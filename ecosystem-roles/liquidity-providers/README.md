# Liquidity Providers

Liquidity Providers (LPs) are the **foundation of ORBT’s yield and liquidity system**.\
They supply assets — such as stablecoins or other supported tokens — into the **ORBT Money Market (ORBTMM)**, enabling continuous liquidity for redemptions, settlements, and cross-chain intents.

In return, LPs earn yield generated through:

* On-chain money markets (Aave v3, Compound-style yield engines).
* Protocol fees and facilitator performance spreads.
* Revenue redistribution mechanisms determined by governance.

Liquidity Providers benefit from **real yield** sourced from actual economic activity, not emissions — making ORBT’s liquidity layer both sustainable and scalable.

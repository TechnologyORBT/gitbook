# ORBT Money Market - Liquidity Custodian

The **ORBT Money Market (ORBTMM)** functions as the liquidity custodian for the ORBT protocol.

It manages **deposits, reserves, and yield generation** while maintaining instantaneous liquidity for redemptions, intent-based settlements, and cross-chain transfers.

Through its tight integration with [**Pockets**](../pocket/)**,** [**Allocators**](../uce/concepts/allocators/)**, and the** [**Unified Collateral Engine**](../uce/) **(UCEs)**, ORBTMM transforms idle capital into productive yield - without compromising settlement speed or user redemption guarantees.

# Governance Incentives & Participation

The **ORBT token’s primary role** is governance within the **ORBT DAO**, but it also has utility functions that strengthen ecosystem alignment.

#### **Governance Voting**

ORBT holders can **propose and vote** on key protocol parameters, such as:

* Fee rates and savings yields.
* Collateral types and UCE updates.
* Facilitator onboarding and bucket capacities.
* Treasury and partnership allocations.
* Cross-chain deployments and security upgrades.

Governance can occur **on-chain** or **via Snapshot**, with execution through a **Timelock** for security.\
Voting thresholds and quorums will be defined (e.g., 1% proposal, 4% quorum).

The large token supply enables **wide community participation** — even smaller holders can collectively shape outcomes.

#### **Staking, Rewards & Slashing**

Staking transforms ORBT into a **productive asset**, enabling holders to earn a **share of protocol revenue**.\
Stakers may receive a derivative like **xORBT/sORBT**, which accrues yield.

Staked tokens may have **lock periods** (e.g., 2–4 weeks) to ensure alignment and discourage short-term manipulation.\
In extreme cases, **slashing** may apply if facilitators or governance decisions cause protocol losses — for example, redirecting future rewards to re-collateralize deficits.

#### **Facilitator and Access Stakes**

Facilitators and institutions may be required to **stake ORBT** to access protocol privileges or higher mint capacities.\
Governance could also require ORBT locks for **custom integrations or committee participation**, reinforcing alignment and accountability.

#### **Fee Benefits and Discounts**

Holding or staking ORBT may provide **discounts on fees** or **enhanced yields** — for example, lower Pocket execution fees or tiered yield bonuses (e.g., 1M ORBT = 10% fee discount).\
This model mirrors exchange tokens like **BNB**, encouraging token holding for utility benefits.

#### **Collateral and External Utility**

ORBT could be accepted as **collateral** in the UCE (under high ratios) or integrated into other DeFi ecosystems for lending and yield strategies.\
Externally, it can serve as a **backstop asset** or **collateral option** in partner protocols, expanding its liquidity and utility.

#### **Governance Alignment & Delegation**

ORBT governance may include **vote delegation**, allowing smaller holders to delegate voting power to trusted representatives.\
Proposals will follow structured discussion → voting → timelocked execution for transparency and security.

#### **Economic and Regulatory Alignment**

The tokenomics prevent concentration of power — initial allocations distribute control among team (15%), investors (15%), and community/DAO (>50%).\
Governance safeguards (e.g., higher quorum for major changes) ensure decentralization as distribution widens.

ORBT emphasizes **utility and decentralized governance**, not speculative profit-sharing.\
Buybacks and burns operate under pre-set, **automated mechanisms**, aligning with decentralized compliance principles.

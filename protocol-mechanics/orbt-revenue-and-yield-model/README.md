# ORBT Revenue & Yield Model

**ORBT’s sustainability** depends on maintaining a **diverse and resilient set of revenue streams**.\
As an active liquidity layer and service provider, ORBT is designed not only to facilitate transactions and strategies but also to **capture value** from those activities in a systematic and transparent way.

The protocol earns income through multiple sources — including **facilitator operations**, **stablecoin stability modules**, **liquidity deployment**, and **governance staking**.\
This diversification ensures that no single market activity dominates the system’s economics, improving long-term resilience and reducing reliance on volatile market cycles.

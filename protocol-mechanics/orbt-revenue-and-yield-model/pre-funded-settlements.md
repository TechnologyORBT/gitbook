# Pre-Funded Settlements

This revenue stream refers to **fees earned from capital placed in advance** to settle future transactions or obligations.\
As described in earlier sections, institutional participants often **lock funds into ORBT Pockets** to guarantee a payment or asset swap at a later date.\
While these funds are held — and, in some cases, temporarily invested — ORBT **charges a service fee** for facilitating the escrow and ensuring execution.

The fee structure can include:

* A **setup or facilitation fee**, applied at the time of deposit.
* A **yield participation fee**, representing a portion of the interest or returns generated while the funds are held.

In effect, **ORBT monetizes the idle escrow period**, transforming dormant capital into a predictable source of protocol revenue.

**For example:**\
A company expecting to pay **1 million USDC** to a supplier in three months deposits that amount into ORBT.\
The protocol issues **0xUSD** against the deposit and invests the funds through safe, short-term yield strategies.\
ORBT then charges a small percentage of the notional amount as a **facilitation fee**, in addition to retaining a share of the generated interest.

These **“intent settlement” fees** can become a major revenue driver for ORBT, particularly in **large-scale institutional flows**.

With over **$20 billion** already processed in such pre-funded transactions, this revenue model has demonstrated both **scale** and **stability**.

It is considered **low-risk**, as the underlying funds remain fully collateralized, and **predictable**, since returns derive from time-value arbitrage on locked capital.

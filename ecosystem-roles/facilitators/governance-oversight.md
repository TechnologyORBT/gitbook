# Governance Oversight

Many facilitators are expected to be **ORBT token holders** or otherwise maintain direct economic exposure to the protocol.\
This **“skin in the game”** approach ensures strong alignment between facilitator performance, governance participation, and long-term protocol success.\
Facilitators may also participate in **specialized committees or working groups** under the DAO, contributing to the continuous refinement of strategies, parameter tuning, and governance policy development.

By operating across diverse strategies and external markets, facilitators significantly **expand ORBT’s reach and functionality**.\
They transform ORBT from a passive liquidity framework into an **active, adaptive liquidity provider** capable of executing market-making, arbitrage, and institutional settlement flows at scale.

This facilitator-driven structure enabled ORBT to **launch with pre-funded strategies and live transaction volume**, establishing early revenue momentum and ecosystem credibility.\
The model builds upon precedents set by **multi-facilitator architectures** in protocols such as **Aave’s GHO** and **Angle’s modular framework**, extending their concepts beyond minting to encompass **comprehensive strategy management and capital deployment** across the ORBT ecosystem.

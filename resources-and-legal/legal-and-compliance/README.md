# Legal & Compliance

While ORBT is fundamentally a decentralized protocol governed by its community, it operates in a context that requires careful consideration of legal and regulatory factors. This section outlines the terms of service for using the protocol, the disclaimers for users and participants (to clarify risks and responsibilities), and how ORBT DAO approaches compliance and jurisdictional issues. Note that this section is not legal advice but provides transparency about the project’s stance and measures.

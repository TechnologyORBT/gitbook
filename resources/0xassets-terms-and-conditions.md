# 0xAssets Terms and Conditions

### 1. Overview

The ORBT Protocol issues a family of blockchain-based assets known collectively as [0xAssets](../orbt-design/0xassets.md), which include but are not limited to:

* 0xUSD, 0xBTC, 0xETH, and other asset-pegged tokens designed to maintain a 1:1 relationship with corresponding reference assets; and
* [s0xAssets](../user-flow-and-guides/how-to-buy-s0xasset.md), representing staked yield-bearing versions of 0xAssets.

These tokens are created and redeemed through ORBT Protocol smart contracts under the conditions outlined below.

### 2. Minting and Redemption

* Users may swap or redeem 0xAssets by interacting directly with approved ORBT smart contracts.
* Certain minting or redemption paths may be subject to eligibility or verification criteria (e.g., whitelisting, compliance checks).
* Minting and redemption are executed autonomously on-chain; results are final and irreversible.
* The Protocol reserves the right to adjust parameters (fees, limits, collateral sets, redemption windows) via governance or upgrade mechanisms.

### 3. Backing and Reserves

Each 0xAsset is designed to be fully backed by reserves held within on-chain contracts or externally integrated protocols. The composition, policy, and balance of such reserves are transparent but may vary over time. ORBT Protocol does not guarantee that backing mechanisms will always maintain full peg value due to market volatility, smart-contract failure, or liquidity conditions.

### 4. Staking and Yield

Users may stake 0xAssets to receive corresponding s0xAssets that accrue yield derived from protocol treasury operations, reserves, and integrated yield strategies. Yield is protocol-targeted and variable, determined by policy parameters and market conditions. No fixed or guaranteed return is promised or implied.

### 5. Risks and Acknowledgment

By minting, redeeming, or holding 0xAssets or s0xAssets, you acknowledge that:

* Smart-contract, oracle, and market risks exist.
* Yield may fluctuate and could be zero or negative.
* You may lose some or all of the value of your holdings.

### 6. No Fiduciary Relationship

Use of 0xAssets does not create any fiduciary, partnership, or advisory relationship between you and the ORBT Protocol or its contributors.

### 7. Modifications and Upgrades

The Protocol may modify or upgrade 0xAssets, or associated contracts without prior notice. You are responsible for monitoring such changes through official channels.

### 8. Disclaimers

0xAssets and s0xAssets are experimental digital assets. No representation is made regarding future value, or liquidity. The ORBT Protocol does not guarantee redemption or convertibility outside of on-chain mechanisms powered by ORBT

# Facilitators

**Allocator** are a cornerstone of ORBT’s extensibility and serve as the operational bridge between the protocol’s liquidity infrastructure and specialized strategies. A facilitator is typically a **team, organization, or entity** with expertise in areas such as **market making**, **arbitrage**, or **asset management**, seeking to leverage ORBT’s unified liquidity layer to execute strategies at scale.

Facilitators operate within the framework defined by **ORBT governance**, which appoints and regulates them to ensure that only **reputable, technically capable, and compliant actors** can assume this role. Their participation enables the ecosystem to expand strategically without compromising security or decentralization.

This section details the facilitator lifecycle, including the process for **onboarding and approval**, their **permissions and operational scope**, and the mechanisms through which **facilitator activities are monitored and managed** within the ORBT protocol.

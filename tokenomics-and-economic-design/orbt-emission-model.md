# ORBT Emission Model

ORBT has a **fixed total supply** at genesis, with **no inflationary minting**.\
What’s referred to as “emissions” is the **scheduled release** of allocated tokens (e.g., community incentives, staking rewards) — not new minting.

This creates a **deflationary bias** over time as burns may outpace unlocks.

#### **Emission Schedule Overview**

* **At TGE:** \~20% circulating (community programs, minor investor unlocks).
* **Year 1:** Team cliff ends; circulation rises to 30–40%.
* **Years 2–4:** Team, investor, and community unlocks increase circulation to \~60–70%.
* **Year 5:** Most tokens unlocked; active burns likely reduce effective supply (e.g., 90B total if 10B burned).

#### **Category Vesting Timelines**

* **Team/Advisors:** Unlocks from month 12 → full at month 48.
* **Investors:** Unlocks within 6–36 months depending on tranche.
* **Community Incentives:** Weighted toward first 2 years for bootstrapping.
* **Staking Reserve:** Linear over 5 years (if used).
* **Treasury/Partnerships/Unallocated:** Locked; spendable via DAO governance.

#### **Transparency Commitments**

All token locks and vesting schedules will be **publicly viewable on-chain**.\
The team will publish wallet addresses and timelines, allowing real-time community monitoring.\
Major unlock events (like team cliffs) will be **announced in advance**.

#### **Market Dynamics**

At launch, ORBT’s market price reflects its circulating supply.\
As unlocks occur, **buybacks and burns** can offset supply increases.

For example:\
If 1B ORBT are distributed in a month but 0.5B are burned, the **net increase** is 0.5B.\
Sustained usage and burns could make ORBT **net-deflationary** over time.

#### **No Mining or Inflationary Rewards**

ORBT does **not rely on PoW/PoS inflation** for security or validator payments.\
It operates on established chains like **Ethereum**, meaning no token issuance for block production.\
Stakers earn from **protocol revenue**, not inflation - mirroring a **dividend-based model** rather than inflationary rewards.

# Token Roles & Circulation

### **Total Supply**

ORBT has a fixed total supply of **100 billion (100,000,000,000)** tokens.\
This large supply ensures ample granularity for micro-transactions and broad distribution — allowing many users to engage with small token amounts and simplifying proportional governance power calculations.

This is the **maximum cap**; ORBT is not expected to mint beyond this amount except through a governance-approved decision in extreme scenarios, such as protocol recapitalization.\
Due to the **burn mechanism**, the circulating supply is designed to **decrease over time**, making ORBT **deflationary** as the protocol matures and generates surplus.

### **Initial Distribution**

The 100B ORBT tokens are allocated across several categories to balance development funding, reward early contributors, ensure community control, and incentivize future growth.

#### **Distribution Plan**

* **Community & Ecosystem Programs – 30% (30 billion)**\
  Reserved for the community, including liquidity mining programs, user incentives, grants, and strategic partnerships.\
  This allocation rewards users providing liquidity to 0xUSD pools, hackathon participants, developers, and early adopters.\
  Tokens are emitted gradually through programs (e.g., a 4-year decay schedule — 10B in year 1, 7B in year 2, 5B in year 3, 3B in year 4).
* **DAO Treasury – 20% (20 billion)**\
  Held by the **ORBT DAO’s treasury**, these tokens serve as a long-term reserve for future initiatives, development, or strategic growth.\
  They are **time-locked** and can only be utilized via governance proposals, ensuring transparency and community control.
* **Team and Advisors – 15% (15 billion)**\
  Allocated to the founding team, core contributors, and advisors.\
  Tokens are **vested over 4 years with a 1-year cliff**, aligning incentives with long-term protocol success.\
  This allocation also covers employee pools for future hires.
* **Investors and Backers – 15% (15 billion)**\
  Reserved for early investors and strategic partners, vested over **1–3 years**.\
  Transparency will be maintained by publicly listing investor allocations and lockups.
* **Staking Reserve & Incentives – 10% (10 billion)**\
  Dedicated to supplementing staking rewards and early governance participation.\
  In early phases, this ensures stakers receive incentives even before significant protocol revenue develops.\
  If unused, tokens may later be **burned or reallocated** by governance.
* **Strategic Partnerships – 5% (5 billion)**\
  Set aside for protocol partnerships, integrations, and market-making agreements.\
  Tokens are **locked and released** based on milestones and partnership terms.
* **Future Unallocated – 5% (5 billion)**\
  Reserved for future use cases or unforeseen needs.\
  The DAO can determine its purpose later — or burn these tokens if unused.

This structure ensures a **community-first allocation** — with over **half the supply** earmarked for ecosystem growth and staking.\
The team and investors’ 30% allocation remains within the standard range for major protocols, reinforced by **long lockups**.

Unit bias (low nominal price per token) could attract retail interest, but ORBT’s primary focus remains **utility and governance**, not speculation.\
Burn mechanics and locked reserves further support **deflationary pressure** and **circulating supply reduction** over time.

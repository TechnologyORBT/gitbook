# 0xUSD Peg Stability & Liquidity Backing

The ORBT tokenomics design ties closely with the stability and liquidity of **0xUSD**, the ecosystem’s stablecoin.

Revenue derived from **PSM operations**, **Money Market utilization**, and **Facilitator profits** ensures that 0xUSD remains **fully backed and liquid**.\
The DAO can deploy portions of ORBT’s treasury toward maintaining **peg stability**, **cross-chain liquidity**, and **PSM replenishment**.

This interlinked model between ORBT and 0xUSD ensures that governance, liquidity, and stability mechanisms reinforce one another — driving long-term resilience and trust in the ORBT ecosystem.

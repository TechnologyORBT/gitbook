# What is it?

[**Aave**](../what-is-a-money-market/how-aave-works.md) **credit delegation** lets a [pocket ](../)(the **delegator**) that holds aTokens **authorize** another address (the **delegate**) to borrow without posting collateral. The debt is denominated in the borrowed asset (e.g., USDC) and is **secured by the delegator’s collateral**.
